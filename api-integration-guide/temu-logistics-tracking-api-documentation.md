# TEMU Logistics Tracking API Documentation

## 

API Overview

**API Name**：[temu.track.trackinginfo.get](https://partner.temu.com/documentation?menu_code=fb16b05f7a904765aac4af3a24b87d4a&sub_menu_code=15dd36e5ed2940379d2275b57b1fbf21)

**Description**： Query logistics tracking information for TEMU platform orders

**Use Case**： Called when ERP system needs to obtain real-time logistics status of TEMU orders

## 

Logistics Status Enumeration

****

| group | logistics Status | State corresponds to event（中文） | State corresponds to event（EN） |
|---|---|---|---|
| In Transit | Pre-shipment | 预上网节点 | Initial scanning before the package is officially shipped and tracked by the carrier. |
| Notification | 尾程物流商的信息通知 | Information update provided by the final delivery carrier. |
| Last-Mile Manifest | 尾程服务商生成的call label信息预报 | Advance information of a call label generated by the last-mile service provider. |
| In transit by carrier | 服务商交邮前的转运节点，例如交邮上网前的“卡转”等；是在尾程物流商官网无法查询到的节点内容 | Movement of goods before being handed to the postal service, usually not visible on the last-mile carrier's official tracking. |
| Local Logistics provider ask for an extra charge | 尾程物流商向消费者收费 | The end delivery carrier requests an extra payment from the recipient. |
| In transit | 货物交邮后，到最后一公里派送点的一切非异常状态 | All normal statuses after the goods are handed to the postal service until they reach the last-mile delivery point. |
| In transit - Exception | 货物从清关完成后，到尾程派送点的一切异常状态 | All exception statuses occurring after customs clearance is completed and before reaching the last-mile delivery point. |
| Last Mile Carrier Picked up | 尾程服务商首次揽收上网 | The initial pickup and scanning of the package by the last-mile service provider. |
| Last Mile Carrier Pick up failed | 取件失败，客户（例如半托管商家）跟揽收人说不寄了等揽收失败的情况 | The pickup attempt was unsuccessful (e.g., customer canceled the shipment). |
| Picked up by Pick-up Carrier | 揽收服务商从卖家/仓库处取货，确认货物已被提走。注意：服务商实际操作可能为扫描大箱而非逐个包裹扫描，若商家填写的大箱、包裹信息不准确，可能存在轨迹多传或少传。 | The pickup service provider picks up the goods from the seller/warehouse and confirms that the goods have been picked up.Note: In actual operations, the service provider may scan master/outer cartons instead of scanning each package individually. If the merchant provides inaccurate master carton or package information, tracking events may be over-reported or under-reported. |
| Inbound by Pick-up carrier | 尾程段，货物进入揽收服务商仓库 | Goods have entered the warehouse of the pickup carrier in the last-mile segment. |
| Outbound by Pick-up carrier | 尾程段，货物从揽收服务商仓库出库 | Goods have departed from the warehouse of the pickup carrier in the last-mile segment. |
| Pick Up Failed by Pick-Up Carrier | 揽收服务商发起揽收后，非服务商原因导致的揽收失败 | Pickup failure caused by reasons not attributable to the service provider after the pickup service provider initiates pickup. |
| Pickup Carrier Handover | 揽收服务商出库后将包裹转运至卡转服务商仓库完成交付。 | After completing outbound, the pickup service provider transfers the packages to the truck transfer service provider’s warehouse to complete handover. |
| In International Transit | 半托管包裹跨国运输中 | A semi-managed package is currently being transported across international borders. |
| Handed Over to Last-Mile | 将包裹交付尾程服务商 | The package has been transferred to the final delivery service provider. |
| Manifest | 非尾程服务商生成的call label信息预报 | Advance information of a call label that was not generated by the last-mile service provider. |
| Arrived at Departure Station | 到达目的站台 | The goods have arrived at the destination terminal. |
| Picked up by Customs clearance company | 清关前清关承运商提货(清关服务商回传的提货节点) | Goods are collected by the customs clearance carrier before the customs clearance process begins. |
| Import Customs Clearance Success | 进口清关完成放行 | Import customs procedures have been finished, and the goods are cleared for release. |
| Customs clearance Exception | 清关延误 | There is a hold-up or delay in the customs clearance process. |
| Import Customs Handover | 清关承运商回传的货物交付节点 | The point at which goods are delivered, as reported back by the customs clearance carrier. |
| Picked up by Truck Carrier | 清关放行后、尾程揽收前，卡转服务商提走货物 | After customs release but before last-mile pickup, goods are taken by a truck transfer service provider. |
| In Transit via Truck | 卡转服务商完成提货后，货物卡转发出到卡转交货前的正常运输状态 | Normal in-transit status after the truck transfer service provider completes pickup, from dispatch of the goods by truck transfer until before truck transfer delivery/handover. |
| Received at Destination Warehouse | 半托管包裹转运到达目的仓库入库 | A semi-managed package has been transferred and received into the destination warehouse. |
| In transit after Customs Release | 清关放行后、尾程揽收前的转运环节 | The movement of goods after they have been released by customs and before they are picked up for the last-mile delivery. |
| In Delivery | Arrived at Post Office | 货物到达尾程派送点 | The goods have reached the final delivery point (e.g., local post office or distribution center). |
| Out for Delivery | 货物从尾程派送点，到客户签收前的一切非异常状态 | All non-exception statuses from the last-mile delivery point until before the customer's sign-off. |
| Delivery Exception | 货物从尾程派送点，到客户签收前的一切非客户拒收的异常状态（不确定是否已尝试派送） | All exception statuses (excluding customer rejection) from the last-mile delivery point to before sign-off (uncertain if delivery attempted). |
| Delivery Failure-Other | 派送中/后无法明确是用户/收件人责任方、无法明确是服务商责任方、未包含责任方的派送失败（经服务商确认，无法归责至任何责任方） | Delivery failure during or after delivery where it cannot be determined whether the responsible party is the user/recipient or the service provider, and no responsible party is specified (as confirmed by the service provider, it cannot be attributed to any responsible party). |
| Delivery Failure-Non carrier | 派送中/后明确是用户、收件人责任方导致的派送失败（责任方主体：收件人、收件方、用户、顾客、收货人、取件人） | Delivery failure during or after delivery clearly caused by the user/recipient (responsible parties: recipient, receiving party, user, customer, consignee, pickup person). |
| Delivery Failure-Carrier | 派送中/后明确是服务商责任方导致的派送失败（责任方主体：快递员、司机、配送员、邮递员、派送员、承运商、派件员） | Delivery failure during or after delivery clearly caused by the service provider (responsible parties: courier, driver, delivery personnel, postal carrier, delivery worker, carrier, delivery clerk). |
| Attempt Delivery | 货物已经尝试送达客户，但不确定失败原因 | A delivery attempt was made to the customer, but the specific reason for failure is not clear. |
| Delivery Failure | 货物已经尝试送达客户，但客户拒绝、地址不详、电话联系不到等派送失败的状态 | Delivery was attempted but failed due to reasons like customer refusal, insufficient address, or inability to contact the recipient. |
| Delivery appointment confirmed | 货物到达派送阶段后，用户要求指定时间或者更改地址派送 | After the package reaches the delivery stage, a specific delivery time or address change is confirmed per customer request. |
| Delivery Failure-Carrier | 由于服务商原因造成的派送失败，例如damage | Delivery failed due to reasons attributable to the carrier, such as damage to the package. |
| Return to sender | 包裹发生退件的场景 | The scenario where the package is being returned to the sender. |
| Return to sender-Other | 派送中/后无法明确是用户/收件人原因、无法明确是服务商原因、未包含原因的退件（经服务商确认，无法归责至任何责任方） | Return during or after delivery where it cannot be determined whether the reason is user/recipient-related or service provider-related, and no reason is specified (as confirmed by the service provider, it cannot be attributed to any responsible party). |
| Return to sender-Non carrier | 派送中/后明确是用户、收件人原因导致的退件（责任方主体：收件人、收件方、用户、顾客、收货人、取件人） | Return during or after delivery clearly caused by user/recipient-related reasons (responsible parties: recipient, receiving party, user, customer, consignee, pickup person). |
| Return to sender-Carrier | 派送中/后明确是服务商原因导致的退件（责任方主体：快递员、司机、配送员、邮递员、派送员、承运商、派件员） | Return during or after delivery clearly caused by service provider-related reasons (responsible parties: courier, driver, delivery personnel, postal carrier, delivery worker, carrier, delivery clerk). |
| The undelivered package is returned and Available for Pickup | 未妥投的包裹发生退件并送到海外仓附近自提点，等待仓库提取 | An undelivered package is returned and placed at a pickup point near the overseas warehouse, awaiting retrieval by the warehouse. |
| Available for Pickup | 货物送到自提点，等客户提取 | The goods have been delivered to a pickup point and are waiting for customer collection. |
| Delivered | Delivered | 签收状态 | The package has been successfully delivered and signed for by the recipient. |
| Delivery Failure | Carrier accepted the claimed of Missing Parcel | 包裹已经丢失，例如lost、stolen | The package is confirmed to be lost or stolen. |
